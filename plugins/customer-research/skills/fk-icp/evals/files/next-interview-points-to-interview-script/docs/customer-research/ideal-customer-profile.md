## Profile

Individual freelance photographer working as a sole trader. Does their own admin, uses a
shared Google Sheet for mileage and Word for invoices. Leaves the tax return to January.

## Pain points

### Last-minute tax-return reconciliation
- **Quote:** "I'd left it all to the last week. I had to go back through eight months of bank statements to work out which lens hire was for which client" (Marcus, 2026-04-14)
- **Type:** practical, emotional
- **Severity:** high (paid £200 to have it fixed)
- **Evidence:** spontaneous

#### Possible Solutions
- Match payments to shoots as they arrive
- Monthly reminder to reconcile

### Losing track of who has paid
- **Quote:** "Invoices I do in Word and I lose track of who's paid." (Marcus, 2026-04-14)
- **Type:** practical
- **Severity:** medium
- **Evidence:** spontaneous

#### Possible Solutions
- Invoice tracking with paid / unpaid status

## Goals, decision-making, budget

- Wants to do the books monthly ("I swore I'd do it monthly this year. I haven't.")
- Decides alone. Has paid £200 once for a relative to sort the books.

## Buyer personas

### January-Panic Marcus
Sole-trader photographer who leaves the books until the deadline. See pain points
"Last-minute tax-return reconciliation" and "Losing track of who has paid".

None yet.
