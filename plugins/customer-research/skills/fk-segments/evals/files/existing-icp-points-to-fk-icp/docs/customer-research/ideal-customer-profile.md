## Profile

Full-time freelance photographers (weddings, events, commercial), working alone, 2 to 10 years in.

## Pain points

### Chasing late client payments
- "Two clients paid late and I couldn't match the payments." (Sam, 2026-04-20, spontaneous)
- Type: practical, emotional. Severity: high.
- Possible Solutions: automatic reminders; payment links on invoices.

### Last-minute tax reconciliation
- "I'd left it all to the last week." (Omar, 2026-06-15, spontaneous)
- Possible Solutions: monthly nudges; matching bank lines to shoots.

## Buyer personas
- **Invoice-Chasing Imogen**: wedding photographer, 40+ shoots a year.

## Negative personas
- **The Hobby Shooter**: earns little from photography, will not pay.
