# Interview with Marcus, 2026-04-14

Me: Tell me about the last time you did your tax return.
Marcus: Oh god. January. I'd left it all to the last week. I had to go back through eight months of bank statements to work out which lens hire was for which client, because two clients paid late and I couldn't match the payments. I ended up paying my sister-in-law, she's a bookkeeper, £200 to sort it out over a weekend. I swore I'd do it monthly this year. I haven't.
Me: What do you use now?
Marcus: A Google Sheet my mate made. It's fine for mileage. Invoices I do in Word and I lose track of who's paid.
