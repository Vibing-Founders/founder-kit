# Ranking segments

Score each group from the broad scan on three things, 1 (low) to 3 (high). Keep it simple: the
point is to choose who to talk to first, not to build a model. Every score carries a short
reason, labelled **sourced** (with a link) or **hypothesis**. At the scan stage most scores
are hypotheses; that is fine, as long as it is said.

## The three criteria

| Criterion | 3 (high) | 2 | 1 (low) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Pain intensity**: how often it hurts, what it costs them, what they already do about it | Hits weekly or more, costs real money or stress, and they already pay for or build workarounds | Hits now and then, they grumble but cope | Rare, mild, or they barely notice it |
| **Willingness and ability to pay**: budget, who decides, what they pay for today | They already pay for tools or help in this area and decide on the spend themselves | Some budget, but someone else signs off, or spend is irregular | Little spare money, or the buyer is not the person with the problem |
| **Reachability within about three months**: where they gather, and whether the founder has a way in | Gather in named, active places the founder can join or already belongs to; the founder can get 5 conversations within weeks | Findable, but scattered or behind gatekeepers | Hard to find, or reachable only through paid channels or slow partnerships |

Add the three for a total out of 9. Break ties on **pain intensity**, then on **reach**: a
group in real pain will make time for a conversation, and one you can reach gets you there
sooner. Apply the same tie-break wherever the shortlist is cut, and if two groups are still
level, say so rather than ordering them silently.

### Raising reach from the founder profile

If the founder profile lists an audience, network or community that contains a group (a
meet-up group, a newsletter, former colleagues, a trade they worked in), raise that group's
reachability to 3, cite the profile line, and say so in the reason. A warm way in matters
more than anything else for a founder with three months.

## Narrowing to 3 to 6

Keep the top three to six. For every group dropped, give **one line** on why ("Large
agencies: have finance staff and invoicing systems already; pain is low").

## When the founder's favourite ranks low

Say it plainly and early, with the reason: "You named small agency owners first, but they
rank fifth: most already have someone handling invoices (sourced: link). I'd start with
freelance photographers instead." The founder can still choose their favourite, but should
do so knowing what the research suggests. Do not quietly bump a score to please them.

## What not to score

No market size, number of businesses, share of market or revenue. A big group you cannot
reach in three months is worth less to a bootstrapper than a small one you can talk to this
week.
