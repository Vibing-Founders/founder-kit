## Pain points

### Last-minute tax-return reconciliation
- **Quote:** "I'd left it all to the last week. I had to go back through eight months of bank statements to work out which lens hire was for which client" (Marcus, 2026-04-14)
- **Type:** practical, emotional. **Severity:** high. **Evidence:** spontaneous.

#### Possible Solutions
- Match payments to shoots as they arrive

### Invoices sent with mistakes
- **Quote:** "I made it in Word, then realised I'd forgotten the VAT number and had to send it again." (Sam, 2026-06-20)
- **Evidence:** spontaneous. **Severity:** low.

#### Possible Solutions
- Invoice templates with required fields

### Quarterly expense catch-up
- **Quote:** "I type them into a spreadsheet at the end of the quarter, which takes a whole Sunday." (Jo, 2026-07-01)
- **Evidence:** spontaneous. **Severity:** medium.

#### Possible Solutions
- Snap-a-receipt capture
