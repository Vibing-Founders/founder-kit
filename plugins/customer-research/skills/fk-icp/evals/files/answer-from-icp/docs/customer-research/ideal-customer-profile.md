## Profile

Sole-trader photographers (weddings, events, portraits) who do their own admin.

## Pain points

### 1. Last-minute tax-return reconciliation
- "I'd left it all to the last week. I had to go back through eight months of bank statements" (Marcus, 2026-04-14)
- "January is just hell, every year, matching payments to jobs." (Ravi, 2026-07-12)
- "I paid an accountant extra because my records were a shoebox." (Ellie, 2026-07-18)
- "I did it the night before the deadline, again." (Jo, 2026-07-01)
- **Supported by:** 4 conversations. **Evidence:** spontaneous. **Severity:** high

#### Possible Solutions
- Reconcile as payments arrive

### 2. Chasing late payments
- "Invoices I do in Word and I lose track of who's paid." (Marcus, 2026-04-14)
- "Two clients owe me from March and I only noticed in June." (Ravi, 2026-07-12)
- "I hate asking for money, so I just don't." (Ellie, 2026-07-18)
- **Supported by:** 3 conversations. **Evidence:** spontaneous. **Severity:** high

#### Possible Solutions
- Automatic reminders for unpaid invoices

### 3. Quarterly expense catch-up
- "I type them into a spreadsheet at the end of the quarter, which takes a whole Sunday." (Jo, 2026-07-01)
- "Receipts live in my glovebox until I can face them." (Ravi, 2026-07-12)
- **Supported by:** 2 conversations. **Evidence:** spontaneous. **Severity:** medium

#### Possible Solutions
- Snap-a-receipt capture

### 4. Invoices sent with mistakes
- "I made it in Word, then realised I'd forgotten the VAT number and had to send it again." (Sam, 2026-06-20)
- **Supported by:** 1 conversation. **Evidence:** spontaneous. **Severity:** low

#### Possible Solutions
- Invoice templates with required fields

### 5. Wanting receipts categorised automatically
- "Sure, that sounds good." (Marcus, 2026-04-14, in reply to "you'd want it all to sort itself automatically, right?")
- **Supported by:** 1 conversation. **Evidence:** PROMPTED (leading question). **Severity:** unknown

#### Possible Solutions
- Automatic categorisation
