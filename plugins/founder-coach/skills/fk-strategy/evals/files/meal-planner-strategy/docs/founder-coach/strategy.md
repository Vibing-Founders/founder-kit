# Strategy

Last updated: 2026-10-05 · Status: agreed

## Diagnosis

Shift workers eat badly after nights because they are too tired to plan or cook, and generic
meal-planning apps assume a nine-to-five week. We do not yet know which shift workers feel this
enough to pay. Aisha and Tom have about 22 hours a week between them and can reach nurses easily.

## Guiding policy

Start with night-shift nurses, whom we can reach through Aisha's groups, and sell a weekly plan
that follows their rota before building an app.

## Coherent actions

1. Interview nurses about their last week of nights.
2. Put up a £4/month pre-order page in the nurses' groups.
3. Send the first plans by hand from a spreadsheet.

## Wedge

- **Customer:** UK hospital ward nurses on rotating night shifts.
- **Problem they own:** no energy to plan or shop around a changing rota, so food is wasted and takeaways bought.
- **Hero use case:** on Sunday a nurse enters next week's rota and gets a plan and shopping list that puts easy meals after nights.

## Value proposition

Eat properly on nights without thinking about it: a plan that follows your rota.

## Strategic bets

### B1. Night-shift nurses will pay for a rota-based weekly plan
- **Hypothesis:** nurses on rotating nights will pay £4 a month.
- **What must be true:** they plan meals at all; the rota is known a week ahead.
- **How we'll test it:** interviews, then a pre-order page in two nurses' groups.
- **Kill or pivot criteria:** fewer than 10 paid pre-orders by 2026-12-15 → try another group of shift workers.

### B2. A hand-made plan is good enough to start
- **Hypothesis:** a spreadsheet plan sent by email is enough for the first 20 customers.
- **What must be true:** making one plan takes under 20 minutes.
- **How we'll test it:** make plans by hand for the first pre-orders.
- **Kill or pivot criteria:** if one plan takes more than 40 minutes after the fifth customer, Tom builds a simple generator.

### B3. Nurses' groups are enough to find the first customers
- **Hypothesis:** the two groups bring in the first 20 paying customers.
- **What must be true:** the groups allow posts about paid products.
- **How we'll test it:** one post a week for four weeks.
- **Kill or pivot criteria:** fewer than 200 visits from the groups by 2026-11-30 → find another channel.

## Not doing yet

- No app until 20 customers pay (because building first skips the offer test).
- No paramedics or care workers yet (because we can reach nurses most easily).

## Phased path

**Now:** prove nurses pay for a hand-made plan. **Next:** automate the plan. **Later:** an app, and other shift workers.
