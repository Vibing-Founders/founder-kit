# Strategy

Last updated: 2026-10-10 · Status: agreed

## Diagnosis

Freelance photographers find bookkeeping painful and most use spreadsheets.

## Guiding policy

Build the friendliest bookkeeping app for all creative freelancers: photographers first, then
videographers, designers and illustrators.

## Coherent actions

1. Design and build the app with a no-code tool over the next three months.
2. Launch on a product launch site and in three creative communities.
3. Partner with accountants who serve creatives.
4. Start a video channel about money for creatives.
5. Offer done-for-you payment matching to the meet-up group.

## Wedge

- **Customer:** creative freelancers.
- **Problem they own:** bookkeeping is boring and confusing.
- **Hero use case:** keeping track of income and expenses.

## Value proposition

Bookkeeping that creatives actually enjoy.

## Strategic bets

### B1. Creatives want a beautiful bookkeeping app
- **Hypothesis:** creatives will switch from spreadsheets to a well-designed app.
- **What must be true:** design matters to them.
- **How we'll test it:** launch the app.
- **Kill or pivot criteria:** if it doesn't get traction.

### B2. Accountants will refer clients
- **Hypothesis:** accountants want their creative clients to keep better records.
- **What must be true:** accountants will recommend a new app.
- **How we'll test it:** talk to accountants.
- **Kill or pivot criteria:** if accountants aren't interested.
