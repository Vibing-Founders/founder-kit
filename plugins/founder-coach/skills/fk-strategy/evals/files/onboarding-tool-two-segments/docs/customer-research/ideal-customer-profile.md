# Ideal customer profile (v0.2)

Built from 11 interviews (6 agency owners, 5 solo consultants), 2026-09 to 2026-10.

## Small marketing agencies (5 to 20 staff)

- Onboarding a new client takes 2 to 3 weeks of chasing logins, brand files and contracts.
- "We lose the first month's margin chasing passwords." (agency owner, unprompted)
- Two pay a virtual assistant about £600 a month, partly for this.
- Buying decision: the owner, usually within a month; already pays for several tools.

## Solo consultants

- Onboard one or two clients a month; use email and a shared folder.
- "It's annoying but it's an hour, I just do it." (consultant)
- Three of five raised it unprompted, but none spends money on it today.
- Price sensitive: most tools they pay for are under £15 a month.

## Open gaps

- No pricing conversations yet with either group.
