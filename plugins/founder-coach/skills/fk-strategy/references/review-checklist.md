# Critical review

Run this after **every** draft or update, and as the whole job in **Review** mode. Report the
result to the founder in a short section headed "Review"; do not bury it in the document.

## The checks

For each, give a verdict (holds / weak / missing) and one line of evidence, quoting the
strategy where possible.

1. **Coherence.** Do the diagnosis, guiding policy, coherent actions, wedge and bets support
   each other? Look for actions that serve a different customer from the wedge, bets that test
   something the policy has ruled out, or a value proposition aimed at someone else.
2. **Reality.** Does it face the actual challenge and the evidence (customer research,
   interviews, traction, offer tests)? Or is it a goal dressed as a strategy? Check against the
   bad-strategy hallmarks in `strategy-kernel.md`: fluff, failure to face the challenge,
   goals mistaken for strategy, bad objectives. Name the hallmark when you see one.
3. **Resources.** Can this founder do it with the hours, skills and runway in
   `founder-profile.md`? Add up what the coherent actions and tests need. If the profile is
   missing or silent, say what you could not check.
4. **Differentiation.** Why would the target customer pick this over what they do today,
   including doing nothing or a spreadsheet? "No real moat yet" is an honest, acceptable answer
   at this stage; pretending there is one is not.
5. **Focus.** Is there at least one hard "not doing" choice: something tempting the founder
   has decided not to do yet, with a reason? An empty or token "not doing" list fails this
   check.
6. **Testable bets.** There are 3 to 5 bets, and each bet's kill criteria have a number, a
   date or an observed behaviour (see `bets-and-kill-criteria.md`). List any that do not.

## Then

- **Gaps and contradictions:** the two or three that matter most, most important first.
- **Most important open questions:** the ones only the founder can answer. Ask the first one;
  add the rest to the document's open questions.
- **Don't rewrite in Review mode** unless the founder asks. Offer the specific edits instead.
- **Deeper pass:** if the strategy is complete and the founder wants it attacked harder,
  recommend pm-skills' `strategy-red-team` (see SKILL.md for install commands).

Be warm and direct. A weak strategy is normal at the start; say what is weak and what would
make it stronger, then let the founder decide.
