# Ideal Customer Profile: FitFeed meal-prep subscription

The UK addressable market for meal-prep subscriptions is roughly £40m a year, so even 1%
would be a great business.

## Primary Persona

Busy professional, 25-40, gym-goer.

Pain points:
- Doesn't have time to cook healthy meals
- Gets bored of the same food
- Finds macro tracking tedious

## Secondary Persona

Student athlete on a budget.

Pain points:
- Can't afford healthy food
- Lives in halls with a tiny kitchen

## Customer interviews

We have spoken to about eight people at the gym.
