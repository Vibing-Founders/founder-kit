## Profile

Individuals who work rotating or night shifts (nurses, paramedics, warehouse staff), live
alone or in shared houses, cook for themselves. Rota changes at short notice.

## Pain points

### Food wasted when the rota changes
- **Quote:** "On my days off I try to batch cook but then my rota changes and half of it goes in the bin." (Nadia, 2026-06-02)
- **Quote:** "I threw out a whole tray of chilli last week because I got moved to nights." (Leah, 2026-06-20)
- **Type:** practical, emotional
- **Severity:** high
- **Evidence:** spontaneous

#### Possible Solutions
- Plan cooking around the published rota
- Suggest freezer-friendly meals when a shift change is likely

### Eating whatever is easy after a night shift
- **Quote:** "I get home and honestly I just eat cereal or whatever my flatmate left." (Nadia, 2026-06-02)
- **Type:** practical
- **Severity:** medium
- **Evidence:** spontaneous

#### Possible Solutions
- A "cook tomorrow" nudge the day before a night shift

### Fitting family meals around two rotas
- **Quote:** "Between my shifts and my husband's, the kids get fish fingers three nights a week." (Dev, 2026-07-08)
- **Type:** practical, social
- **Severity:** medium
- **Evidence:** spontaneous

#### Possible Solutions
- Shared rota view for a household

## Goals, decision-making, budget, digital habits

- Want to eat properly on nights without spending days off cooking.
- Decide alone (or with a partner for families).
- Has spent about £40 a month on meal kits (Nadia).

## Buyer personas

### Night-Shift Nadia
A nurse on rotating nights who lives in a shared house and cooks for herself. Tries to
batch cook on days off. Plans meals at home on her laptop on her day off.
Pains: see "Food wasted when the rota changes" and "Eating whatever is easy after a night shift".

### The Rota-Juggling Parent
Works shifts, as does their partner; plans meals for children around two rotas.
Pains: see "Fitting family meals around two rotas".

## Negative personas

### The Macro Counter
Wants calorie and macro tracking first; the rota is secondary. Warning sign: asks about
protein targets before mentioning shifts. (Kim, 2026-07-09)

## Qualifying questions

- What happened to the food you planned last time your rota changed?
- Who else do you plan meals for?

## Research gaps

- How far ahead do rotas get published?
- Would they pay for this, and how much have they paid for alternatives?
